Privacy policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

Who this covers

QuickBooks Assistant is a private tool operated by Henrique Abreu for a single QuickBooks Online company that Henrique Abreu controls. It has no other users.

What data is accessed

Using Intuit's QuickBooks Online Accounting API, the tool reads accounting records — customers, items, invoices, transactions and reports — from the one connected company. It can also create invoices in that company.

Where data goes

Data is requested on demand and displayed in the operator's terminal or written to files on the operator's own computer. There is no server, database, or cloud storage operated by QuickBooks Assistant. No QuickBooks data is transmitted to any third party, and none is used for advertising, profiling, resale, or training any system.

Credentials

OAuth 2.0 tokens issued by Intuit are stored in a file on the operator's computer with owner-only permissions, and are excluded from version control. Access tokens expire hourly and are refreshed automatically; refresh tokens rotate on each use. Tokens are never logged, shared, or transmitted anywhere except to Intuit's own token endpoint.

Retention

Exported files persist only until the operator deletes them. Revoking the connection renders the stored tokens useless immediately.
