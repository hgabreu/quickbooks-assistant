Disconnecting QuickBooks Assistant

How to revoke this app's access to your QuickBooks company.

From QuickBooks Online

Sign in to QuickBooks Online, open Settings (gear icon) → Apps → Connected apps, find QuickBooks Assistant, and choose Disconnect.

From the tool

Running ./qb revoke revokes the stored token directly with Intuit.

After disconnecting

Stored tokens stop working immediately and no further data can be read or written. Any files already exported to the operator's computer remain until deleted manually.
