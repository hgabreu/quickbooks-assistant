Connecting QuickBooks Assistant

How access is established.

QuickBooks Assistant connects to QuickBooks Online through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. The operator initiates the connection from the command line, approves it in the browser on Intuit's own consent screen, and selects the company to connect.

To reconnect after disconnecting or after an expired token, the operator repeats that flow. No credentials are entered anywhere except on Intuit's own pages.
