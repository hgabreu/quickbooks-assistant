QuickBooks Assistant

A private QuickBooks Online integration.

QuickBooks Assistant is an internal command-line tool used by Henrique Abreu to read reports, export data, and create invoices in a single QuickBooks Online company. It is not a public product and is not offered to other users.

It runs locally on the operator's own computer. There is no hosted service, no user accounts, and no shared infrastructure.
